Our beginning

A familiar warmth.
A new point of view.

Ember & Spice began with a simple idea: make a place where the food feels familiar, the details feel considered, and every guest is welcomed like an old friend.

Our menu takes inspiration from the many kitchens and regions that make Indian food so wonderfully varied. We cook with the seasons, follow our curiosity and always leave room for one more person at the table.
